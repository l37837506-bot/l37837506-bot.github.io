Highlight 01 · 2026 · Seoul Robot & AI Museum

MediBot

A 30-minute medical robotics programme I deliver to visitors at Seoul Robot & AI Museum, introducing the da Vinci surgical robotic system with microscopy and VR-based learning activities.

Role
Researcher – AI & Robotics Education
Where
Seoul Robot & AI Museum (RAIM)
Format
30-minute programme
Audience
Museum visitors

01 Overview

What the programme is

At RAIM I design and deliver AI and robotics education programmes for learners from age 8 to adult. MediBot is the medical robotics programme among them: a 30-minute session for visitors built around the da Vinci surgical robotic system.

02 Activities

Three ways into medical robotics

  • 01

    da Vinci surgical system

    An introduction to the da Vinci surgical robotic system.

  • 02

    Microscopy

    A microscopy-based learning activity for visitors.

  • 03

    VR

    A VR-based learning activity for visitors.

03 Context

Why it matters to me

Studying Big Data Science alongside Biosystems and Biomedical Sciences got me interested in applying data analysis to healthcare. Medical robotics programmes at work are one of the ways I have been extending that interest.
