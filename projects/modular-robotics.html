Modular robotics programme

A two-hour session of the Gyeonggi Shared School programme for Year 1–2 high-school students in Yangpyeong, built around an extreme-environment exploration mission on the UGOT platform. I led the session from planning to lesson plans.

01 Overview

One session, owned end to end

As part of the Gyeonggi Shared School programme, I took full charge of one session for Year 1–2 high-school students in the Yangpyeong area.

The two-hour modular robotics programme was co-developed with the Gyeonggido Yangpyeong Office of Education and integrates the UGOT platform with an extreme-environment exploration mission.
