Highlight 04 · 2024 · Korea University Sejong Campus

Predicting KOSPI 200 Trends Using Ensemble Models and CNN

Machine learning on KOSPI 200 market data from 2000–2023, comparing four ensemble models with CNN and LSTM. 3rd Place at the Big Festa Project Competition, School of Big Data Science, Korea University Sejong Campus.

Period
Mar 2024 – Jun 2024
Team
With one teammate
Award
3rd Place, Big Festa Project Competition · Dec 2024
Output
Research poster

01 Background

From coursework to competition

This project started as a Statistics and Machine Learning course assignment focused on applying ensemble machine-learning models to a real-world dataset. Together with a teammate, I chose to analyze KOSPI 200 market direction, building on the coursework by extending the project with deep-learning models and preparing it for a departmental competition.

02 Approach

Features and models

We analyzed KOSPI 200 historical market data from 2000–2023 and constructed return- and volatility-based features for market direction prediction. On the ensemble side, we built and evaluated Random Forest, LightGBM, XGBoost and CatBoost, with GridSearchCV for hyperparameter tuning. On the deep-learning side, we implemented CNN and LSTM to capture spatial and temporal patterns in the financial data.

03 Results

MAE, MSE and cumulative returns

Model performance was compared using MAE, MSE and cumulative returns. Among ensemble models, Random Forest performed best; among deep-learning models, CNN outperformed LSTM.

We presented our methodology, results and conclusions on a research poster, which received 3rd place in the competition.

04 Takeaways

What carried over

The project gave me hands-on experience across the full data science workflow — feature engineering, model selection, hyperparameter tuning, evaluation, and communicating findings clearly through visual and written formats.

While it focused on financial data, the analytical techniques transfer directly to the health-data analytics I now pursue at work and through independent study.
