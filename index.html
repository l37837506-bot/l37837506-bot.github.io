About

AI and robotics education, with a growing interest in data analysis for healthcare.

I work as a Researcher at Seoul Robot & AI Museum (RAIM), designing and delivering AI and robotics education programmes for learners from age 8 to adult. My day-to-day work spans curriculum design, teaching materials, robot hardware testing and running programmes with students directly.

I graduated from Korea University in Big Data Science with a second major in Biosystems and Biomedical Sciences. Studying both got me interested in applying data analysis to healthcare — an interest I have been extending through medical robotics programmes at work and a machine-learning project on KOSPI 200 market direction that won 3rd place in a departmental competition.

Beyond my professional work, I have volunteered with community health organizations supporting older adults living alone. That experience shaped how I think about accessible and inclusive services, and it is part of why I am drawn to work at the intersection of data, AI and public benefit.

03 Project

Machine learning on KOSPI 200 market data

3rd

Award · Dec 2024

3rd Place, Big Festa Project Competition

Statistics and Machine Learning PBL · School of Big Data Science, Korea University Sejong Campus

Mar 2024 – Jun 2024 · With one teammate

Predicting KOSPI 200 Trends Using Ensemble Models and CNN

  1. Context

    Started as a Statistics and Machine Learning course assignment on ensemble models. With a teammate, I extended it with deep-learning models and prepared it for the department's Big Festa project competition.

  2. Data & approach

    Built return- and volatility-based features from KOSPI 200 historical market data, tuned four ensemble models with GridSearchCV, and implemented CNN and LSTM to capture spatial and temporal patterns.

  3. Results

    Best results by model group
    GroupModelMSEMAE
    EnsembleRandom Forest0.0050.06
    Deep learningCNN0.00670.069

    CNN outperformed LSTM, and Random Forest led the ensemble models.

  4. Outcome

    The methodology, results and conclusions were presented on a research poster, which received 3rd place. The workflow — feature engineering, model selection, hyperparameter tuning, evaluation and communicating findings — transfers directly to health-data analytics.
